Stellis 0.1.0 is the first public release: a local app that records, transcribes, and organises conversations on your Mac.

**Recording and transcription**
- Record from the microphone or import MP3 files up to 1 GB, with a live transcript while you record.
- English transcription with word timings, on your Mac.
- Speakers grouped by voice. When a speaker sounds like someone you named before, Stellis suggests that name; one click confirms it.

**Reviewing**
- Play from any word, search a conversation, highlight, comment, and edit the transcript.
- Reprocessing keeps your speaker names, edits, highlights, and comments.

**Organising**
- Home groups conversations by type (Meetings, Interviews, Workshops, Briefings, Calls) and shows each group's takeaways.
- Notebooks collect conversations for a project. Search covers every conversation.
- Upcoming events from a connected Google or Outlook calendar.

**AI features, with your Nebius API key**
- Summaries linked to the transcript, answers about a conversation, and automatic sorting by type. Add the key in Account settings; only the transcript text these features need is sent to Nebius.

**Try it**
- `scripts/load_samples.py` adds three meetings from the AMI Meeting Corpus (CC BY 4.0).

**Known limits**
- English only. Speaker groups can be off by one or two people in busy meetings; names you confirm stay fixed.
- Requires macOS on Apple silicon and uv; `uv run stellis` installs everything else. See the README.
