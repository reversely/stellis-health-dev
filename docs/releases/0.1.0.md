Stellis is an app that records, transcribes, and organises conversations, with live local inference on Whisper ASR.  Process your memos with a suite of AI transcript processing tools, knowledge organization & insights when you bring your own API key from Nebius Token Factory.

**Recording and transcription**
- Record from the microphone or import MP3 files up to 1 GB, with a live transcript while you record
- English transcription with word timings; Speakers grouped by voice

**Reviewing**
- Play from any word, search a conversation, highlight, comment, and edit the transcript
- Reprocessing keeps your speaker names, edits, highlights, and comments

**Organising**
- Tag speakers in your conversations
- Search through conversations by title, speaker, or quoted content, and through notes and Summaries
- Categorize your conversations with tags and home Notebooks that can serve as your persistent homebase for information about projects
- Group notebooks into collections; the sidebar shows collections, notebooks, and their meetings and notes, and items drag between them
- Sync upcoming events from a connected Google Calendar

**Notes and to-dos**
- Notes without a recording, alongside meeting notes, in the same Markdown editor
- Typed Markdown shortcuts, callouts, toggles, columns, tables, code, link cards, YouTube, Vimeo, and Loom embeds, images, and GIFs
- To-dos are checkbox lines in any note: type a date in words ("tomorrow at 3pm") to set it, and mark items done or canceled; Home and the pinned To-dos page gather them by notebook
- Copying from a note gives plain Markdown; a notebook exports as Markdown, or as an export for Stellis that keeps task IDs

**AI features, with your Nebius Token Factory key**
- Summaries linked to the transcript, answers about a conversation, a clean transcript, and automatic tags, using NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory
- When a notebook has to-do suggestions on, the Transcript Agent reads each new Summary and suggests to-dos to add or close, each with a quote from the transcript; you edit, accept, or reject each suggestion, and nothing is added to your notes until you accept it
- Only the transcript or note text a feature needs is sent to Nebius; audio stays on your computer

**Try it**
- A short tour starts after you sign up and walks through adding a Nebius key, the sample workspace, and the main screens; run it again from the ⋯ menu on Home
- **Add sample workspace** adds example collections, notebooks, notes, and to-dos, and three meetings from the AMI Meeting Corpus (CC BY 4.0); **Remove sample workspace** removes only what it added
- The user guide in `docs/guide/` lists every feature and keyboard shortcut

**Known limits**
- English only. Speaker groups can be off by one or two people in busy meetings; names you confirm stay fixed.
- Records the microphone; it does not capture other apps' audio.
- Requires uv; `uv run stellis` installs everything else. See the README.
