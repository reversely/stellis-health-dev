# Stellis UI style guide

Use this guide for new screens and when revising existing controls. It records the accepted interface decisions; the [contrast workspace experiment](contrast-workspace-experiment.md) keeps the reference observations and revision history. Inspect any new user supplied reference before changing the related screen, and record what the reference shows separately from design inference.

## Visual foundation

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark purple core | `#2C2234` | Home rail edge, recording rail, auth backdrop |
| Light paper | `#F7F5F6` | Default writing canvas and light card surfaces |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Inputs and the light theme auth card |
| Dark ink | `#2A252F` | Text on paper |
| Muted mauve | `#413A48` | Dark primary controls and the home rail highlight |
| Dark control | `#44364E` | Secondary controls on the rail |
| Teal | `#335C63` | Home rail focal color, waveform, and light theme focus |
| Wine | `#654654` | Recording Stop action |

Use the roles already defined in `src/stellis/static/theme.css` before adding a color. Check text and icon contrast in both themes. Dark mode is dark purple, never black or neutral charcoal. Use `#302638` for the canvas, `#382C42` for cards and inputs, `#44364E` for raised controls, and `#2C2234` for the header and navigation. These shared roles also supply media and call surfaces; do not add local black backgrounds. Keep the light document surface flat. Dark mode keeps the mauve writing canvas, uses solid white primary action pills, and uses white at 80% opacity for glass secondary controls and account surfaces. Glass controls use dark ink. Blur belongs only on overlays with content behind them; a flat canvas needs no blur. Beige is not an action colour.

The **desktop home left rail** is a WebGL fill (`static/rail-shader.js`) of five soft blobs in the user's palette `#495575`, `#56465C`, `#594F4F`, `#547980`, and `#45ADA8` over the dark core. The bright teal blob is smaller than the others. The blobs drift slowly, travel about a quarter of the rail, and lean only slightly toward the pointer. `--rail-blur` on `#rail-shader` sets edge softness from 0 to 1; the default is 0.55 so neighbouring colours blend. Static grain sits in the shader and in an SVG overlay at 30% opacity with an overlay blend. Reduced-motion preferences get one still frame. Without WebGL the rail falls back to the centered radial gradient. Its transparent header is continuous with the rail, with no separate top bar or divider. The recording rail and writing canvas use solid fills. Dark-mode account and overlay surfaces use the shared white glass role. Do not spread the gradient across controls or other pages.

## Type and shape

- The brand is the v2 mark (`static/logo-mark.png`, an alpha mask drawn in `currentColor`) beside the lowercase wordmark in upright Piazzolla, in one colour: near-white on dark surfaces, ink on light ones. Never set the logo in italics.
- Use upright Piazzolla 300 (light), approved by the owner on 2026-10-20, for page headings. Keep the existing regular 400 wordmark; use Inter for controls, labels, and body text. Keep a clear size and weight difference between page title, section title, and supporting text.
- Reserve interface italics for long subhead phrases, never headings or isolated label words. User-authored rich text and the Italic formatting control remain available. Recording titles use the same approved Piazzolla 300 face at every viewport.
- Do not place an eyebrow or overline above a heading. Avoid decorative taglines and explanations that do not help someone act.
- The conversation document is square and attaches cleanly to its notebook tabs. Primary tabs (Notes, Transcript, and the recording page tabs) have a 7px top radius and square bottom corners; the large outward curves are retired. An unselected tab is a quiet raised surface with muted text and a hairline border. The selected tab is inverted (text-colour fill, surface-colour label) and sits on the 2px rule that joins it to the document. `.view-tabs` is the same shape for notebook view tabs. Keep My Notes and Enhanced Notes quieter than the primary tabs.
- **List widgets** (`.list-widget`) are a heading row (`.list-widget-head`) over plain rows (`.list-widget-rows`, `.list-widget-row`) on the page surface. There is no white card box and no fill. A hairline separates one row from the next and appears nowhere else.
- **Dates in lists are plain text, never pills.** Pills are for tags and categories. `formatListDate(isoDate)` in `shared/list-date.js` returns Today, Tomorrow, a weekday within the next six days, or a short date such as "Oct 24" (with the year when it is not the current one). Put the result in a `.list-widget-date` element at the right of the row. The Date format setting in Account settings (Relative, Short date, ISO) is stored per browser in localStorage under `stellis-date-format`, and the formatter reads it.
- **To-do lines in notes** show a date typed in the line ("tomorrow at 3pm") as plain right-aligned text drawn by `formatListDate`, never a pill. A canceled to-do is struck through with a dash in its box. A ⋯ button at the right of the line opens Complete, Cancel, and Reopen; ⌘D completes and ⌘⇧D cancels, and typing `[-] ` at the start of a line cancels it.
- Use small corners for cards and controls: roughly 6px for cards and 8px for ordinary inputs and light-mode buttons. Dark-mode primary actions use white pills; secondary glass actions may share that shape. Do not apply a large radius to every surface.

## Controls and states

- Style every interactive element explicitly. A primary button has a filled surface, clear text, at least a 44px touch target, and a visible hover, keyboard focus, and disabled state. The auth submit button is 48px tall. A secondary button is visibly quieter but still reads as a control. Never leave a browser default button in a finished screen.
- Primary task actions share `--action-fill`, `--action-ink`, hover and disabled roles. Use the same component classes as Record and Add goal. Light mode uses mauve with white text; dark mode uses solid white pills with dark text. Log vital signs, setup recovery and actions in editors follow these roles. Keep wine for destructive actions and retain quieter secondary controls.
- An **inverted** control fills with the theme's text colour and draws its glyph in the colour of the surface behind it, in both themes; it is not a flat accent fill. Summary regenerate is a 44px inverted icon button with no text button beside it.
- The theme switch is a bare 44px sun or moon icon in the header with no border, fill, or text. Take new UI icons from Tabler Icons (MIT, outline, 2px stroke) and record each in `static/icons.NOTICES.txt`.
- Keep Record, Import, Pause, and Stop visually related within the solid recording rail. Use the wine fill for Stop. Do not give Record a competing bright accent.
- **Shared form controls:** `theme.css` supplies low-specificity defaults for text fields, text areas and buttons. Use the existing 8px corners, input fill, field border and visible focus. Single-choice selects use the Tabler chevron-down. Browsers supporting `appearance: base-select` also use themed picker surfaces, 44px option rows and Tabler checkmarks; other browsers retain their platform picker with a styled closed control. Keep native select semantics and change events. Checkboxes use a 24px outlined box and an inverted checked state, with a native forced-colours fallback. Component rules retain layout and intentional title-field treatments. Onboarding uses the dashboard's bare 44px sun/moon theme switch with an accessible name describing the next theme.
- Label inputs directly. Use a semantic `button` with an explicit type when it sits in a form. Icon-only buttons need an accessible name. Errors appear close to the action and describe what the user can do next.
- Show live recording and transcription where the user is working; make interruption and recovery states explicit. Match the status text to the actual recording state.
- **Inference configuration:** Read `ai.configured` from `/api/v1/capabilities` when showing AI actions. Explicit local mode needs no Nebius key; it uses the GN100 runtime and loopback inference endpoint. Cloud mode uses its own credentials and configured gateway. Settings names the profile, distinguishes configured routes from live checks, and offers installed local model switching only to the runtime owner. Saved results remain readable when inference is unavailable. Do not present cloud-key setup as a requirement for local inference.
- **Agent avatars:** `shared/agent-avatar.js` draws every agent's avatar from its ID: a 7 by 7 pixel sprite mirrored about its middle column, in one of eight palette colours. Coordinators (the default agent and notebook stewards) show a white sprite on a filled disc; acting agents show a coloured sprite on a white disc with a hairline ring. The same module serves the Chat list and thread, suggestion cards and the recording AI Chat panel. The avatar is decorative (`aria-hidden`) because the agent name always sits beside it.
- Use short, familiar UI labels. Do not use interpuncts or middle dots (`·`) anywhere in user-facing copy, including metadata. Never mention Otter in the product UI. Show an unnamed speaker as `Speaker N` with no appended prompt.

## Screen hierarchy

- **My Health (`/`):** The default dashboard shows Health Overview, Upcoming, My Notebooks, Measurements and Health History in that order. Health Overview spans the full width. Upcoming and My Notebooks share a row above 760px; narrow screens keep the section order in one column. Previews use existing source data and link to full Summary, Trends, Timeline, Records or notebook views. Upcoming separates scheduled calendar appointments, dated records and saved plans; a future date alone never means booked care. The dashboard shows up to three selected notebooks and measurements and up to five recent history entries. Notebook cards show their actual collection and a recent source title; measurements show their latest value, date and a small chart when at least two readings exist. Customize dashboard saves notebook, measurement and active-care choices per account. Active care uses only notebooks the person selects. Keep the five sections visible with relevant empty states. Goals and library retains search, capture, check-ins, the detailed calendar, notebook management, recent conversations and tags in a collapsed supporting section. Due reminders and incoming check-in calls remain visible outside it. Preserve original record links and notebook names.

- **Global capture:** Add stays available in the desktop toolbar and as a labelled fourth mobile navigation slot. Its modal offers Take Photo, Enter Reading, Upload Record, Record Visit, Add Appointment and Check-in. Notebook pages and Ask Stellis pass their selected notebook; general capture offers general health history. Reuse shared upload, recording, Trends and check-in components. Device-photo values require review before saving, with editable value, unit and date and a retained source image. Check-ins stay in a contextual dialog. Scheduled and planned appointments retain their distinct status. Use shared primary action roles, secondary choices, 80% white glass in dark mode and existing dialog motion. Keep the dialog heading fixed above a scrolling body; Close and Escape restore focus.

- **Home agents:** The Agents widget leads Home, above the columns. It groups agents like the notebooks: each collection, then **Notebooks** for notebooks outside one, then **No notebook**. An agent with notebooks in several collections appears under each. Coordinators lead each group. A chip is a 44px pill with a 36px agent avatar, the name, the notebook names in that group, and a **Steward** tag when it stewards one of them; it links to `/chat/{id}`. Edit widgets can hide it, and the choice persists per browser.
- **Agent settings:** A view in Chat at `/chat/settings`, reached from **Agent settings** beside **New agent** and from **Settings** in an agent's thread; not from the rail or Account settings. It groups agents the same way as Home (`shared/agent-groups.js`). Each agent is a closed row (avatar, name, role, notebook names, **Steward** tag) that opens to its name, role and instructions, its notebooks and steward choice, and its memory, with **Open chat**. The per-agent Setup tab exposes that agent's editable instructions, assignments and memory; the global management view remains separate.
- **To-dos:** One component (`static/todos-widget.js`) serves Home's left column, the pinned To-dos page at full width, and a notebook page (scope: all notebooks, grouped under a notebook header, or one notebook, flat). A heading row carries the Open, Completed, and Canceled filters (icon buttons with small corners, the selected one inverted), then an input row that adds a to-do to the uncategorized note titled "To-dos" (created on first use; on a notebook, that notebook's own note), then plain rows with no card boxes. A row is a checkbox (toggles Open and Completed, and reopens a canceled item), the text, a thin grey source label that links to the meeting, and a right-aligned plain date: the due date for an open item, the completed date for Completed, the canceled date for Canceled. A pending agent suggestion shows as a quiet sparkles label (a link to the source note or meeting, where its card waits) on a faintly tinted row with a straight 2px left edge. The list runs to the end with no truncation; an empty filter shows one line.
- **Suggestions:** Agent suggestions (`static/suggestions.js`) render around the notes editor, never inside its document, in a meeting's My Notes and on a note page. Agents never insert blocks into the notes. When pending suggestions exist, a 300px column of cards stands beside the editor and stacks under it below 900px. The column head holds the count, previous and next (chevron-up, chevron-down; each scrolls to and focuses a card), **Accept all** (double-check icon) and **Reject all** (X icon). A card has a straight edge (border-radius 0), a 24px agent avatar with the agent name ("Transcript Agent", no tag text), the change in words (**Add to-do:**, **Mark done:**, **Cancel:**, **Reopen:** and the line's text), the evidence quote with a clock-icon link that seeks the transcript, and accept (check) and reject (X) icon buttons, all 44px with accessible names. An evidence segment that does not resolve (sample meetings are not transcribed) shows the quote and the link reads "Open transcript", which opens the transcript at its top without an error. Pending `add_todo` suggestions also show as green lines under a "Suggested to-dos" label below the editor, each editable in place (`PATCH /suggestions/{id}` on a pause or blur) before it is accepted. A pending `set_todo_status` suggestion tints the matching to-do line in the editor, found by its text, without changing the document. After an accept the notes are read again so the server's lines and IDs appear. A queued or running Transcript Agent run shows "Transcript Agent is reading this meeting" above the editor; other states show nothing.
- **Workspace setup:** Welcome opens one personal question at a time with a short upward transition. Text Stellis opens the shared text conversation beside the active question. Typed or spoken answers fill an editable answer and ask for confirmation before saving. Clarifications and pauses keep the question open; manual edits and unsent drafts survive replies. Field advancement waits for queued speech to finish. Name comes first, followed by gender, period status, birth date, conditions, medicines, plural health goals and multiple wellness interests. Cycle interest follows an explicit relevant answer. Period status uses the shared health preference from Settings; setup creates no cycle history. Review offers editable notebooks before explicit creation. Choices survive reload and Back in account-scoped server state. Reopened setup preserves existing notebooks and records. Reduced motion removes field travel and typing animation.
- **Notes:** A note (the UI never says "page") opens at `/page/<id>` from the sidebar, a notebook card, search, or New note on Home. Under the back link, a title row carries the file icon, an editable serif title (saved on Enter or blur) and a trash button that moves the note to Recently deleted after a confirmation. Below, one row holds a notebook select (notebook icon, with No notebook) and a tag pill for every tag (tag icon, small 4px corners; a selected pill is inverted), each saving at once. The formatting toolbar and the shared notes editor follow, with the save status beneath. Typing `/` on an empty line or after a space opens the slash menu (`static/notes-slash.js`) in every notes editor: a 320px card at the caret (flipped above near the bottom edge, at most the viewport width less 16px) with sections Basic blocks, Layout and Media, a Tabler icon and the Markdown shortcut at the right of each row, filtering as you type, arrow keys and Enter to insert, and Escape to close and keep the typed slash; Image appears only where the editor can upload images. The editor saves one PUT per edit with the expected revision, keeps a local draft, merges on conflict, adopts server-assigned to-do IDs, and sends typed dates as `todo_dates`. Images paste or upload into the note; an image pasted from another note, meeting, or recording is copied so the note owns it. The body is one column up to 820px and fits a phone with no horizontal scroll.
- **Account settings and notebook pages:** Settings groups Profile, Sign-in, Custom vocabulary, Date format, Integrations, and Delete account, each under an icon tile. Primary actions are inverted buttons; Delete account uses the wine fill. The notebook page is documented in its own bullet below.
- **Navigation:** My Health and Ask Stellis are the only primary global destinations. Summary, Trends, Records and Timeline remain available through My Health and retain their existing URLs. The sidebar shows active notebooks the person has created or selected, grouped under their actual collections; empty collections and source rows stay out of this navigation. Collection groups collapse and remember their state. Add Notebook opens the curated selector. On phones and tablets up to 1000px, a fixed bar provides My Health, Ask Stellis, Notebooks and Add. Notebooks opens the same tree in a labelled modal; Escape restores focus to the trigger. Settings remains available at the foot of the tree. Notebook preview choices on the dashboard do not archive or remove notebooks from navigation.
- **Notebook Ask workspace:** Follow `docs/refs/stellis-demo/notebook-ask-sources-viewer.png`: tabs directly below the notebook header, then aligned Sources, Chat and source viewer panels. Do not insert the Records/Ask/Check-ins summary tiles between the header and tabs. The desktop notebook rail is 280px; Sources is 240px, the viewer takes 29% (at least 280px), and Chat keeps the flexible reading space. Below 1384px the viewer becomes a sheet, leaving the two-column answer readable; phones stack Sources and Chat. The composer follows the complete answer in document flow and never covers citation checks or follow-ups. Keep the sheet's record link above mobile navigation.
- **Notebook page:** `/notebook/<id>`. Under the back link, a header band tinted with the notebook colour at 24% opacity over the page (so it does not clash with the dark sidebar or the light page) holds a breadcrumb, `collection / notebook`, with a folder icon on the collection (plain text, as collections have no page) and the notebook icon and serif name as the last crumb. At its right: the primary **New note** (note icon; creates a note in this notebook and opens `/page/<id>`) and **Record** (microphone; records into this notebook) buttons and a ⋯ menu with **Add a conversation** (plus), **Settings** (gear), **Export Markdown** (markdown icon), **Export for Stellis** (file-zip icon) and **Delete** (trash). Both exports call `GET /api/v1/export?scope=notebook&id=<id>&format=markdown|stellis` and download the ZIP. Below sit the `.view-tabs` **Overview** (dashboard icon) and **List** (list icon). Overview holds the To-dos widget scoped to this notebook (its input appends to this notebook's "To-dos" note) beside **Latest summary** (sparkles; the Summary overview of the newest meeting that has one, shown unmodified under a link to that meeting; the section is omitted when no meeting has a summary), then **Notes and meetings**. List shows only the items table. The items table has columns for an icon (microphone for a meeting, file for a note), the title, a plain right-aligned date from `formatListDate`, tags as pills (the only pills on the page), and to-do counts (open circle and completed check-circle, each with a number). The All, Meetings, and Notes filters are small-corner icon buttons, not pills. No status labels are invented. Under 640px each row becomes a grid: icon, title and date on the first line, tags and counts on the second. **Settings** opens a dialog with the name, the colour (the twelve presets from `GET /notebooks` as swatches, plus a custom field that takes `#RRGGBB` and a native colour picker), the collection, and **Suggest to-dos from meetings**: `Like <collection>` (or `Off` when the notebook has no collection), `On`, `Off`, saved with `PATCH /notebooks/{id}` `todo_list`. Card text keeps the better house ink when it reaches 4.5:1 contrast; custom colours use full white or black when neither house ink reaches that level. Dates retain that ink at full opacity. The **+** on a Home notebook card opens a dialog with **New note**, **Record**, and **Import**, then the list of conversations to move in.
- **Recording:** Keep capture actions and status in the dark rail; give the light notebook or transcript the wider reading area. Notes and Live transcript are the primary tabs.
- **Coming up notebook:** Each event has a Notebook action beside Notes and Record. Its dialog chooses a notebook or No notebook, with Apply to this series for recurring events. The chosen notebook name appears under the meeting title and Record carries that notebook into the recording.
- **Saved conversation:** Attach the primary file tabs to the square document. Keep transcript reading, seeking, notes, and related actions in predictable positions.
- **Authentication:** Use a centered paper card on the dark mauve backdrop. Make Sign in and Sign up clear, give the submit button the same deliberate shape and states as other primary actions, and support the light theme.

## Before merging a UI change

1. Inspect each named reference and relevant existing screen state. Recheck earlier feedback for the same workflow in [the reference ledger](otter-ui-reference.md) and [the contrast experiment](contrast-workspace-experiment.md).
2. Compare the rendered result in a browser at desktop and narrow widths, in both themes where available. Check the relevant default, hover, focus, disabled, loading, error, and alternate states. Check for horizontal overflow and page errors.
3. Verify behavior as well as appearance: tab changes, live updates, status transitions, and form submission where touched. Record observed results separately from inference and update this guide if an accepted design decision changes.

## First-run setup

The first-run screens follow PDF pages 7–9 by workflow, while retaining the shipped fonts, logo, Tabler icons, generated agent avatars and the rich sidebar shader. Welcome and uses have no library rail; reviewed proposals introduce it. Tablet and phone layouts move the surrounding welcome icons into a stable grid. Scene changes use a 220ms opacity/4px transition, with movement removed by reduced motion; there is no continuous decorative icon motion.

Local setup explicitly says Completely local inference and names GN100. Cloud setup names Nebius Cloud + Token Factory separately. Readiness requires a live check, and errors use the server response. Starter notebooks are labelled as based on choices; illustrative wireframe counts and schedules are never presented as real data. Use tiles expose pressed state and retain stable accessible names. Existing accounts bypass first-run setup.

- **Agent conversation workspace:** Chat reuses the full shader library rail, with agent identities above the collection tree. The active agent has Thread / Setup / Work tabs, Call beside its identity, a separately scrolling conversation and a persistent bottom composer. Notebook chips belong to individual messages; the composer scope uses a familiar select. Unsent text and selected scope survive agent switches and reload within the current browser session, keyed by account and agent. Submission appears immediately; real job state is polled without implying token streaming, and the next unsent draft is not replaced when a reply arrives. Control/Command+Enter submits. The context panel displays real output behavior, assignments, memory and named copies. Existing draft/delegation tools live in Work. On phones the agent picker is at the top and the library follows the conversation. The broader reviewable-artifact workflow remains tracked in #437; notebook-row agent management remains #438.

## Camera readings

Home and Trends group pulse, breathing rate and heart rate variability under **Vital signs** with one primary **Log vital signs** camera action per group. Home shows the latest values together; Trends keeps their charts and tables in the same section. Manual entries use the quieter **Log manually** action. Blood pressure, sleep and weight keep separate logging. Camera goals and the capture heading use **Log vital signs**. The capture explains the available readings before starting; it saves only estimates the recording can produce.

## Transcription setup

The Home setup card reports the shared speech-model installer. Waiting state offers Start setup. Downloading shows actual bytes for the current model and uses a determinate bar only when the response supplies its size. Connecting and checksum verification use an indeterminate bar. Failure offers Try again; unavailable status offers Check again. Ready state hides the card. Avoid estimated total sizes or a percentage when the installer cannot measure progress.

## Interaction motion

Use motion for changes the person requests. Menus enter over 160ms from their trigger; dialogs and backdrops enter together over 220ms and leave over 180ms. The call panel enters over 200ms with 8px of travel. Newly opened forms use 220ms with 4px of travel. Use `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` for these transitions.

Primary task actions acknowledge pointer presses at scale 0.97 over 120ms. Keyboard interactions are immediate. Reduced-motion preferences remove scale and travel while retaining short opacity and colour fades. Avoid broad page entrances, decorative loops and animated row arrivals. Theme changes apply in one frame.

## Health catalogue

Add Notebook opens a searchable catalogue shared by onboarding and the workspace. Approved health collections appear in their four groupings. Each option shows its curated name and scope; recommendations quote the editable intake notes. People can select several templates or create a custom notebook within an approved collection. Creation requires Add notebooks. MedlinePlus attribution stays available in the catalogue footer. Navigation shows collections containing active notebooks. Notebook settings retain user names and descriptions and support reassignment and archiving. Add Notebook includes restoration of archived notebooks. Existing legacy organization remains intact until the person changes it.


## Onboarding speech recovery

The call distinguishes Listening, Hearing you and Processing your answer. Done speaking uses the existing button style and appears while the microphone can accept an answer. The minimized call hides this control with its other detailed controls. Its compact panel stays beside the notes on wide screens and above them on narrower screens so it cannot cover the notepad. Unclear recognition shows a repeat-or-type action; an accepted answer clears that message. Voice failures retain the call and its typed answer field.


## Call and tour completion

Calls share status labels and expose Retry reply after a preparation failure. Add records follows onboarding; Go to My Health follows a completed tour or check-in. My Health keeps Daily check-in beside Call Stellis. Each tour stage has a visible title and narration. The tour uses the selected surface behind its narration card to separate it from the page. Its conversation region scrolls while the header and controls remain visible. Phone message fields use 16-pixel text and occupy a separate row above the controls.

## Check-in agenda

The incoming check-in card stays a filled block with inverted ink. It shows when it rang, Answer, Chat instead, the snooze (*In N minutes*, from the server) and Not now, then *On this call*: each agenda item with its icon, title and source label. In the call, the agenda becomes a checklist with *n of m* progress. The first line carries *Written from your goals and records*. When Stellis proposes an entry, the log shows *The model chose* with the tool and the person's words, followed by the inline review with Log selected and Cancel. Message bubble styles apply only to message text, so review rows inside the log keep their own styling. The person's bubble uses the block roles, so it stays dark on the light glass panel in dark mode.

## Onboarding call continuity

The call panel beside the live Notes/Transcript notepad is the primary onboarding interface. Keep Start call visible at entry, with Text Stellis as an alternative. Preserve the avatar, live status/ring, timer, mute, speaker, end, answer-finish, retry and minimize/expand controls. New confirmed-answer fields belong inside Notes; they must not replace this call interface with a standalone questionnaire. Notes stay editable and confirmation remains explicit. Compact mode stays in the layout beside or above the notepad.


## Period day logging

The Cycle daily log follows the supplied Apple Health reference: weekday and date labels above pale vertical capsules, with coral circles for recorded flow. Each day is an accessible toggle that immediately saves `Had flow`; a second tap removes that observation. Save failures restore its prior state. Days save independently for consecutive taps. Keep detailed flow choices optional, week navigation and a date picker for history, and the existing shared Calendar projection.

## Chat attachments

Ask Stellis and notebook chat share Attach beside the composer. PDF and image files appear as filename links with a notebook selector and Save to notebook. Saved files link to their notebook. Needs text links to the retained document for correction. Remove applies to unsent chat attachments and retains the original record. Upload and send states disable conflicting controls. Attachment rows wrap on phones and keep filenames readable. Notebook Records includes Documents alongside Meetings and Notes.

## Original report assistance

The full document page uses the original report as its primary surface. Selecting a recognized line highlights it and requests an explanation; subsequent questions retain that source selection. Keep explanations alongside the page on desktop and below it on phones, with page/zoom/rotation controls and keyboard-accessible source regions. Source chips open the relevant report page or selected region. Historical explanations remain readable when inference is unavailable; changed evidence is labelled. Extracted text and correction controls sit in a supporting disclosure rather than leading the experience. See [report evidence](evidence/148/README.md).

## Shared style roles and audit

Use `theme.css` as the colour and geometry catalogue. Component styles reference roles rather than raw colour or corner values. Keep notebook/calendar colours supplied by saved data, fixed media palettes, status colours and original-report highlights distinct from task-action colours.

| Family | Shared roles and dimensions |
| --- | --- |
| Typography | `--font-body` (Inter), `--font-heading` (Piazzolla); detail page titles share `--text-page-title`. |
| Spacing | `--space-1` through `--space-12` follow 4px steps; `--space-compact` is the deliberate 6px icon/text gap. Ordinary former 10/14/18px component gaps use 12/16/16px. |
| Page gutters | `--page-padding-block`, `--page-padding-inline`, `--page-padding-bottom`: 28/32/48px on ordinary desktop pages, 16px top/inline on narrow pages. Rail views retain their grid placement; onboarding/auth retain their standalone composition. |
| Controls | `--control-height` is 44px, shared primary padding is 10px by 16px and control gap is 8px. Auth submit remains 48px; capture choices remain 56px and fill equal grid columns. Phone fields retain at least 16px text. |
| Corners | `--radius-control` 8px, `--radius-panel` 6px, `--radius-dialog` and `--radius-message` 12px, `--radius-tab` 7px, `--radius-pill` fully round. Small text highlights use the separate small/text roles. Circles remain percentage geometry. |
| Primary actions | `--action-*` supplies fill, ink, hover, disabled and radius; reuse its padding and minimum target. Dedicated inverted regeneration icons use the local text/surface pair. |
| Secondary actions | `--secondary-*` supplies surface, text, border, hover, disabled and radius. Dark mode uses the accepted white glass family. Document actions, chat attachments and manual trend actions follow it. |
| Contexts | `--rail-*` and `--paper-*` preserve fixed dark rails and fixed paper contexts. Glass/paper surfaces recompute disabled/status/chevron roles locally so dark-page tokens cannot make their text or controls illegible. |
| Focus | `--focus-width` and `--focus-offset` are 2px. Focus colour follows the actual surface; original report regions use the fixed-paper source role. |

**Colour blocks** carry inverted ink. `--block-fill`, `--block-ink` and `--block-muted` serve selected tabs, pressed filters, accepted tags and the citation open in the viewer. `--block-alert-*`, `--block-ok-*`, `--block-calculated-*` and `--block-planned-*` serve status blocks (`.status-block` with `is-alert`, `is-ok`, `is-calculated` or `is-planned`). Light pages use dark fills with light ink; dark pages flip to light fills with dark ink rather than darkening the block. Dark-mode glass overlays (dialogs, menus and the call panel) are light surfaces, so their blocks and primary actions take the light-page pairing: a dark fill with light ink instead of a white pill on white glass. Every pair reaches 4.5:1 on its surface, which `tests/browser/ui_consistency.mjs` checks on the page and inside a dialog in both themes.

Home and Trends use the same `--data-series-*` palette for the same measurements. Chart, camera, call, cycle and pillar colours live in the catalogue under semantic names. Report hit regions preserve their actual source coordinates instead of taking a 44px UI target; native 24px checkboxes retain the larger clickable label around them.

Run `python3 scripts/audit_ui_styles.py` before introducing component colours or corners. CI enforces this rule for shipped component CSS and excludes vendor-owned assets. `tests/browser/ui_consistency.mjs` checks the actual workspace at 390px and 1280px in both themes, including target sizes, phone field text, solid-surface contrast, secondary states, capture geometry, focus return and overflow. `tests/browser/style_sweep.mjs` additionally checks primary actions, auth/onboarding and motion/reduced-motion behavior. Gradient rail contrast requires visual inspection; a solid CSS colour calculation does not represent the shader.

## Trends organization

Keep a stable measurement hierarchy regardless of whether readings exist: Vital signs (pulse, breathing rate, heart rate variability, blood pressure, temperature), Body measurements (weight), Blood sugar (blood sugar, HbA1c), Sleep, and Symptoms (pain). Empty measures are quiet rows inside their category, with secondary logging actions; never collect unrelated measures under “Start tracking”. Recorded measurements remain inside the same category after saving. Vital signs retain one camera action; manual entry is secondary.

Reading metadata uses a semantic date and a separate source label (“Manual entry”, “Camera reading”, or the source record title), without conversational fragments or comma-joined sentences. Comparisons with personal history are neutral information, not red/green severity alerts. Retain the shared teal series and usual-range band across charts.

Mobile navigation stays fixed at the viewport bottom, with safe-area padding and enough page space to scroll the final action fully above it. Review viewport captures at the top, middle and bottom of long pages. A full-page stitched screenshot places a fixed element at one viewport boundary within a taller image; it cannot establish how navigation behaves during scrolling.
